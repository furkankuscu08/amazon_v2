## Repository map

`workbot/` is the application package, `tests/` contains offline regression tests, and `docs/` holds design and competition research. Key paths:

- `compose.yaml` runs local Qdrant and, optionally, the app; `.env.example` is the configuration template, while `.gitignore` excludes secrets and generated files. `requirements.in` lists direct dependencies; `requirements.txt` is the compiled Python 3.12 dependency lock.
- `workbot/main.py`: FastAPI routes and server-rendered flow.
- `workbot/models.py`: strict structured-output models and schema validation. Each `Area` carries a `consultation_need`; each `PersonProposal` carries a `primary_evidence_id` (must be one of its citations), a required 5-300-character `contribution`, and a `relevance` of `direct`/`adjacent`/`weak`. Primary/contribution precede role in the requested field order. Contribution is an unverified factual scope/need bridge, not a semantic verifier; never use its length, persuasiveness, or wording to infer direct support. Keep the direct-contact evidence requirement in the audit, not the schema, so one inconsistent role field cannot void a whole area review. Missing/invalid contribution in a live reply remains a schema failure, not a field to repair.
- `workbot/service.py`: session orchestration, bounded concurrent per-area model calls, evidence audits, and card states. Keep SQLite/search/permission reads and evidence audits on the request thread; only independent area model requests belong in workers. Wait for all areas before the card-wide priority call, keep area failures isolated as `review_incomplete`, and discard an unsafe shared question without discarding a valid priority order. Check the question before reordering so a guard error leaves the original card order intact. An advisor may cite a current visible responsibility for a narrow question; manager/remit or title alone still cannot justify that role. A `direct_contact` means the person worked directly on this area's `consultation_need`; locally, only a strong work or current-responsibility tie on the model's single `primary_evidence_id` can be verified. A proposal the model marks `adjacent`/`weak` is kept as an advisor, and `mention`-only evidence is not proposed. These checks verify visibility and link strength, **not** area-specific semantic relevance. An otherwise eligible proposal with a malformed question is omitted, not rescued; with no accepted contact it yields `review_incomplete`, while an actual empty/unsupported review remains `not_found`. Accepted model questions are format-checked but replaced on the card with role/source-type-neutral questions. Keep the primary evidence visible first and never place diagnostic rejection details in the card passed to priority.
- `workbot/search.py`: visible BM25/Qdrant retrieval and directory candidate selection.
- `workbot/questions.py`: shared immutable neutral English question registry with no model/schema/DB imports. The area prompt asks for an exact choice based on role and primary-source relation; `_grounded_ask` still selects the displayed question from verified evidence. Do not turn ask into an enum, add a template-membership rejection rule, rescue malformed asks, or treat a neutral question as proof of role/need relevance. Audit ignores model contribution text; keep it out of cards, sessions, priority, and logs. No raw reasoning narrative is requested.
- `scripts/historical_models.py`: explicit offline-only contribution adapter used by quality/audit replay. Missing captured values get the documented unobserved/unscored sentinel on in-memory copies; explicit values, even invalid ones, are preserved and locally validated. Never change immutable captures/labels, score a placeholder as observed output, or use this adapter for live LLM replies. The audit does not read the field, so historical role/citation/status metrics must remain unchanged.
- `docs/evaluations/20261012-main-vs-combined/` and `tests/test_handoff_comparison.py`: immutable, byte-identical synthetic area-comparison bundle with offline integrity/context/label-boundary checks. Original absolute paths are provenance only; use relative bundle paths for inspection. Never rerun the saved dispatch, interpret old approval as a new budget, or turn post-review labels into observed model output. Preserve all summaries, original asks/contributions, primary sources, and provisional/pending labels separately. This does not authorize live replay or prove semantic correctness.
- `scripts/quality_eval.py` and `tests/fixtures/quality_baseline.json`: offline replay of sanitized live observations against fresh synthetic fixtures, not a general quality guarantee. Five borderline baseline labels were adjudicated by the user; any future pending labels must not count toward scores. `tests/fixtures/quality_scenarios.json` contains approved synthetic references and one-run target-area observations, not replayable full search/model traces. In candidate selection, title matches without visible evidence must not expose restricted-only people to model context.
- `scripts/trace_diagnostics.py`, `tests/fixtures/quality_live_traces.json`, `tests/fixtures/quality_trace_labels.json`, and `tests/test_trace_diagnostics.py`: separate offline diagnostics of six synthetic DeepSeek/Nitro runs, captured before the current card/status changes. The experimental model is not the shipped `workbot/llm.py` default. Keep the captured observations immutable; assistant-reviewed source-based labels live separately, are **not user-adjudicated**, and leave borderline person ties pending. For evaluation, an advisor may explain a related real process/work item to help understand the need or approach the appropriate practitioner; area expertise is not required, but a concrete source-to-need bridge is. Do not assume the person knows a particular owner or bypass manager/remit-only routing rules. Judge usefulness independently of direct claims, malformed asks, and question premises; an accepted advisor label does not erase an unsupported direct claim or original ask. Impact hypotheses are not automatically false just because the idea or work sources do not mention them. Preserve each run's generated consultation need and original model ask; do not silently transfer labels from the older area needs or score pending per-person labels. Person plus a correct **primary** source may be preserved as direct or advisor, while unsupported model/card direct claims and model/old-card question premises are separate failures. These traces are observations, not a full rerun or a model-quality guarantee.
- `scripts/trace_audit_replay.py` and `tests/test_trace_audit_replay.py`: offline audit-only comparison of those captured proposals against the current service, rebuilding candidate pools from the captured visible record IDs and search intents. This does not rerun retrieval or the model; compare audited roles/citations and status transitions, not semantic quality. Do not mistake this for a changed model output or add diagnostic metadata to cards passed to priority.
- `tests/fixtures/quality_low_traces.json` and `tests/fixtures/quality_low_labels.json`: three later isolated synthetic HTTP runs with DeepSeek V4.1 Flash `:nitro` and requested low reasoning, plus separately reviewed assistant labels of their newly generated area needs and proposals under the same advisor rubric. The rubric is user-approved, not the per-case judgments; subagent participation is not independent human validation. Use `python -m scripts.trace_diagnostics --traces ... --labels ...` or `python -m scripts.trace_audit_replay --traces ...` offline. Do not reuse the high-run labels for changed needs, mistake source-based assistant judgments for user approval, or change the shipped OpenRouter default based on these three trials. Two low-run area replies also hit the 8,192-token cap; a card being produced is not proof that every area was reviewed.
- `workbot/storage.py`: SQLite schema, visibility, sources, and reload snapshots.
- `workbot/llm.py`: three structured OpenRouter model tasks, pinned to a tested parameter-compatible provider endpoint with local schema validation. The area prompt separates primary-source facts, personal link, useful contribution, and role; preserve the distinction between specific-need direct work and process-explaining advisors without requiring area expertise. Do not invent a known practitioner, borrow strength from another source, or downgrade historical work merely because of age. Questions may seek an unknown answer but must not assert undocumented controls, duties, findings, or contents. The small combined-branch live comparison does not isolate the baseline prompt or individual contribution/question/effort effects; instruction/transport tests and replay of older proposals do not measure their semantic effect. Do not treat a strict JSON-schema request as proof that the provider enforces local cross-field rules; do not log raw provider bodies or silently accept malformed responses.
- Model-call diagnostics in `workbot/llm.py` are best-effort logger-only `model_call_summary` events (`INFO` success, `WARNING` failure), with call-local task/outcome/category, monotonic duration, HTTP status, allowlisted finish reason, and bounded non-negative integer usage. Missing usage stays `null`; do not coerce booleans/strings, recompute totals, infer reasoning usage, or interpret provider counts as proof of the reasoning setting's effect. Keep prompts, provider text, validation inputs/context, exception messages/tracebacks, identity/source/session/area IDs, and headers/secrets out of logs. Do not log the diagnostic writer's own failure or let it mask the call result. Preserve the existing `ModelError` contract and never add this metadata to cards, stored sessions, or priority inputs. This instrumentation does not fix truncation or authorize live calls, retries, provider fallbacks, or budget changes.
- `validation_error_types` is logger-only: sorted unique allowlisted Pydantic codes, unknown codes mapped to `other`, and `null` outside local validation failures or on extraction failure. Never log error locations/indices, messages, inputs, or validator context, or turn a raw custom error type into a log label. Custom rules remain generic `value_error`; do not infer the exact rule or add schema restrictions from this diagnostic. Extraction failure must preserve safe `ModelError` behavior (generic `schema_invalid` fallback). The four-started-call GLM probe retained only three results; the fourth provider outcome/usage is unknown after harness interruption, not an HTTP timeout or token failure. Do not retry it without new live-call approval or retroactively attribute the earlier schema failure.
- `scripts/area_probe.py` and `tests/test_area_probe.py`: isolated, opt-in, one-area synthetic observation harness with offline preparation/inspection. Production diagnostics remain logger-only; this separate runner may persist only their validated allowlisted summary, never raw provider/error/validation text or headers/secrets. Freeze the context with `prepare_manifest`, preserving visible candidate/evidence order against a fresh seed and prompt/LLM/schema hashes; do not point it at real/demo data. Obtain separate live approval before `run --approve-live`; implementing/testing the runner grants none. Use one standalone process per observation and a background job with adequate lifetime, not a shared foreground batch deadline. An exclusive new directory claim and pre-call attempt record prevent duplicate reuse; do not delete/rename these to retry. A summary is saved in the logging callback, a visible schema-valid response before audit, and audit separately. Pre-call persistence failure prevents invocation; post-call failure must not retry or discard already saved observations. A started call without a summary consumes one invocation and has unknown provider outcome; `inspect` reports artifacts, not process death or semantic quality. Failure events can be emitted by the model wrapper's `finally` during interruption: confirm failure/category only with a separately saved caught-`ModelError` marker; retain unconfirmed summaries without treating them as completed errors. Count starts across directories against the separately approved limit; the CLI has no global budget/auth enforcement. Preserve app settings/states and immutable captures; do not retroactively fill the lost GLM result.
- `workbot/seed.py`: small synthetic fixtures and index seeding.
- `workbot/templates/`: English server-rendered UI and source views.
- `tests/test_workflow.py`: session states, evidence audits, and visibility rules; `tests/test_search_and_ui.py`: hybrid search and HTTP flow; `tests/test_llm.py`: model request and response contracts; `tests/test_quality_eval.py`: offline replay stage attribution and visibility checks. These are offline tests.
- `docs/competition/`: `rules.md` is a local extract (the live rules supersede it); `alexa.md` distinguishes the two Alexa+ routes; `submission.md` is the submission checklist. Read its judging, mini-challenge, and prizes notes only when relevant. See the document roles and competition context above for the other root-level and `docs/` files.

## Working with the user and other agents

- Inspect the working tree and relevant tests before editing; preserve existing user changes. Do not modify competition rules under `docs/competition/` unless the user explicitly asks.
- If anything about the request, requirements, scope, or intended behavior is unclear, **ask the user before proceeding rather than guessing**. Do not silently turn a roadmap idea into current behavior.
- Delegation is optional and is the main agent's decision. Use the built-in `explore` subagent for focused discovery or `general` for useful independent multi-step work. Independent tasks may run in parallel; dependent tasks may run sequentially. Communicate with subagents in English and integrate and verify their findings yourself. See `opencode.jsonc` for their configuration.
