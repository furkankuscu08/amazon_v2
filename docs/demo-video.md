# Demo video script (draft)

**Status: working draft, not final.** Written on 14 October 2026, restructured on 19 October 2026, and rewritten on 20 October 2026 for the new saved plan, the judges' reviews, and an opening on three problems at company scale, then shortened the same day; the user will revisit it. Any line, timing, or decision below may change. Recording and editing come after this script is agreed.

The story is told on the **saved marketing-spend plan** on the hosted demo's home page (session `6f661e79…`, made with Amazon Nova 2 Lite on 20 October 2026, the first attempt after the impact-prompt change; it was kept because it passed the answer key, asked no question, and showed Omar with the ended-role note, which 7 of 10 runs measured before the directory hints did), so the video says word for word what the Devpost text quotes and what a judge sees on opening that plan. A **separate live run** of another idea then shows the app working end to end. People, questions, and wording vary from run to run, so the live segment is written after it is recorded, and nothing from it is presented as the saved plan or the other way round.

## Decisions so far

- The hook comes first: the video opens on Omar's ended role next to Tara's current one, before any product introduction. This contrast is the moment the judges' reviews said a viewer remembers.
- Right after the hook, the narration names three problems at company scale: work done twice, nobody knowing who to ask, and work with no owner. The video then shows one moment for each: Omar and Tara on the saved plan, related work in the live run, and the open area on the saved stablecoin plan (shown as WorkBot saying where it found no one, not as proof that the work is unowned: Legal and Compliance has a manager whose remit covers compliance). The Devpost text's "At company scale" section uses the same three moments.
- The proof of Omar's ended role is the directory record, so the video opens the **Source** link on the ended-role note, not the policy document. It does this before any follow-up, so going back to the plan loses nothing.
- "Prep me for Tara" stays out of the video: its answer restates what the card already shows.
- The live run comes right after the saved plan, at about 1:09, so the working app is shown before the second half; the open area follows it.
- Whenever a saved plan is on screen, the caption reads "Saved plan from an earlier version · new runs can differ".
- Two voices: a narrator, and the app's own voice (Amazon Polly reading the plan summary and follow-up answers), recorded from the app itself. The narrator never speaks over the app's voice. Who narrates is decided at recording (the user's own voice, a different Polly voice, or captions only); every narration line below also works as an on-screen caption. Maya is never heard: **Speak** is fed a synthetic voice sample, captioned as such, and the video shows the box filling.
- About 2:36 in all, kept under 2:40 to leave room in the edit, with about 186 narrated words (about 2.5 words a second, counting each year as two words); the app's voice takes about 23 seconds on the saved plan (the first three sentences of the summary and "Why Omar?"), about 7 on the stablecoin plan (an estimate until recorded), plus the live run's.
- The live segment runs customer scoring: Noah Baker came first in all 5 measured runs with directory hints, so "Why Noah?" can show work already done. The home page already holds a saved plan for that idea, so the live run repeats it; its plan may differ from the saved one, and the video does not compare the two or show the saved customer-scoring plan. If Noah is not on the live plan, show "Why Noah?" on the saved customer-scoring plan instead, with its caption, and do not label the live run as Noah's.
- Ideas whose stored cards showed a wrong direct contact (pickup lockers, campaign AI) stay out of the video.
- Claims stay within what the code does: code checks record visibility and the person's link to it, not whether a record answers the area's question; the card shows no model-written summary of a person, and code assembles every sentence Alexa says in the plan summary and follow-up answers; the only model-written text on the page is the area names, what to find out, the shared question, and a clarification question. Each person's evidence line links to its record; "What to find out" has no source.
- No gendered pronouns for any person, Maya included.
