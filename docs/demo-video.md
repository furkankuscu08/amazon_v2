## Decisions so far

- The hook comes first: the video opens on Omar's ended role next to Tara's current one, before any product introduction. This contrast is the moment the judges' reviews said a viewer remembers.
- Right after the hook, the narration asks the three questions a new idea raises in a large company, in the order the video answers them: who handles this now (Omar and Tara on the saved plan), is someone already working on it (related work in the live run), and who is missing (the open area on the saved stablecoin plan, shown as WorkBot saying where it found no one, not as proof that the work is unowned: Legal and Compliance has a manager whose remit covers compliance). The Devpost text's "Three questions it answers" section uses the same three moments. The narration names them as questions, not as findings: it never says that work was done twice or that any work has no owner.
- The proof of Omar's ended role is the directory record, so the video opens the **Source** link on the ended-role note, not the policy document. It does this before any follow-up, so going back to the plan loses nothing, and the reload lands at the top, beside the suggestions.
- "Prep me for Tara" stays out of the video: its answer restates what the card already shows.
- The live run comes right after the saved plan, at 1:09, so the working app is shown before the second half; the open area follows it.
- Whenever a saved plan or one of its source pages is on screen, from the first frame, the caption reads "Saved plan from an earlier version · new runs can differ".
- Two voices: a narrator (Google Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS, the voice Kore, called through OpenRouter; the user chose it over the Polly standard voices, the only ones the demo's Region offers, which sounded mechanical), and the app's own voice (Amazon Polly, Joanna, reading the plan summary and follow-up answers), taken from the app's own audio for the same text. The narrator never speaks over the app's voice (at least 1 second apart), and every voice is mixed at the same loudness. Two versions are cut, one with the narration as burned-in subtitles and one without; the user picks one. The labels (saved plan, synthetic voice sample, live run) are in both. Maya is never heard: **Speak** is fed a synthetic voice sample, captioned as such, and the video shows the box filling.
- 2:35 in all, with about 187 narrated words; the app's voice takes 23.5 seconds on the saved plan (the first three sentences of the summary and "Why Omar?"), 5.4 on the stablecoin plan, and 18.4 in the live run.
- The live segment runs customer scoring: Noah Baker came first in all 5 measured runs with directory hints, so "Why Noah?" can show related work already assigned, and Noah was first on the recorded live plan too. The home page already holds a saved plan for that idea, so the live run repeats it; its plan may differ from the saved one, and the video does not compare the two. If a re-recorded live plan has no Noah, show "Why Noah?" on the saved customer-scoring plan instead, with its caption, and do not label the live run as Noah's.
- Ideas whose stored cards showed a wrong direct contact (pickup lockers, campaign AI) stay out of the video.
- Claims stay within what the code does: code checks record visibility and the person's link to it, not whether a record answers the area's question; the card shows no model-written summary of a person, and code assembles every sentence Alexa says in the plan summary and follow-up answers; the only model-written text on the page is the area names, what to find out, the shared question, and a clarification question. On the cards, each person's evidence line links to its record, except a routing contact's; "What to find out" has no source.
- No gendered pronouns for any person, Maya included.
